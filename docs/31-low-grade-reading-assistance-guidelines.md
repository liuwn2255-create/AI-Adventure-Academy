# Grade 1–2 Reading Assistance Guidelines

This student-facing reading assistance mode applies only to `grade-1` and `grade-2`. Grade 3–6 keep their existing presentation.

- Prefer short, familiar sentences and one task at a time.
- Use concrete, everyday wording. Avoid abstraction when a direct action can be named.
- Tell students what to do and how many items to choose when the task requires a fixed count.
- Keep the question and answer choices visually distinct.
- Student text may include manually reviewed pronunciation tokens. Tokens are optional metadata; the original plain text remains authoritative.
- Offer on-demand listening for the task prompt. Never autoplay, and send only the plain prompt text to the shared speech service.
- Do not add pronunciation marks to every page control or teacher-facing detail.
- Do not generate readings at runtime. Do not bulk annotate activities without editorial review.

These are presentation and instructional design guidelines. They do not define scoring, answer keys, or submission rules.
